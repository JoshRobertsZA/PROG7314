## Full description (1716/4000)

Waypoint keeps your whole trip in one place, even when you have no signal.

PLAN EVERY DAY
Create a trip with your destination and dates, then build it day by day. Add flights, hotels, car rentals and places to visit, attach your booking PDFs, and change dates or add notes whenever plans change.

TRACK YOUR FLIGHTS
Enter a flight number and Waypoint shows its status, route, departure time, terminal and gate for the day you are travelling.

SMART REMINDERS
Waypoint watches your trips for you: countdowns before you leave, a nudge if a trip has no plan yet, a summary of each day's plans, a warning the night before an early flight, and an alert if bad weather is coming. Tap a reminder to open the trip.

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO
Check the weather at your destination, convert between 21 currencies with live rates into your chosen home currency, and read a short summary of the places you are visiting.

DISCOVER NEARBY
Find restaurants, parks, pubs and cinemas near you or near your destination, and add the ones you like to a day of your trip.

WORKS OFFLINE, SYNCS WHEN YOU ARE BACK
Your trips are saved on your phone, so they open without a signal and you can keep editing. When you reconnect, changes sync to your account automatically and are restored on a new phone. Your settings follow you too.

STAY UP TO DATE ACROSS DEVICES
Create a trip on one phone and get a notification on your others. Trip updates and reminders can be switched on or off separately.

BUILT FOR YOU
• Sign in with Google, GitHub, or an email and password
• Unlock the app with your fingerprint, face or PIN
• Light and dark themes
• Available in English, isiZulu and isiXhosa

Waypoint is free, has no ads and does not sell your data.
