# Play Console: Content rating and app content answers

Answers for Play Console > Policy > App content. The content rating questionnaire is run by IARC and gives a rating for each region (ESRB, PEGI, USK, ClassInd and others) from the same answers.

## Content rating questionnaire (IARC)

**Category:** Utility, Productivity, Communication, or Other (a travel planner, not a game, social or news app).

| Question | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Does the app contain violence (realistic, fantasy or cartoon)? | No | Trip planning only |
| Blood, gore, or depictions of injury? | No | |
| Sexual content, nudity or suggestive themes? | No | |
| Profanity or crude humour? | No | All text is written by us or comes from Wikipedia summaries |
| References to, or use of, drugs, alcohol or tobacco? | No | Explore lists pubs as places, but the app does not promote or show alcohol use |
| Simulated or real-money gambling? | No | |
| Fear, horror or disturbing content? | No | |
| Can users interact with or exchange content with other users (chat, comments, uploads)? | No | Each user's trips are private to their own account |
| Does the app share the user's current location with other users? | No | Location is only sent to LocationIQ and OpenWeatherMap to find nearby places and weather |
| Can users buy digital goods? | No | No in-app purchases |
| Does the app give unrestricted access to the internet (a web browser)? | No | It only calls the APIs listed in the privacy policy |
| Is the app a news app? | No | |

**Expected rating:** Everyone (ESRB), PEGI 3, USK 0, ClassInd L, IARC 3+.
