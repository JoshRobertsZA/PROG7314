## Full description (1372/4000)

Waypoint is a travel itinerary planner that keeps your whole trip in one place, even when you are offline.

PLAN EVERY DAY
Create a trip with your destination and dates, then build it day by day. Add flights, hotels, activities and notes to each day, and attach your booking PDFs so tickets and confirmations are always at hand.

TRACK YOUR FLIGHTS
Enter a flight number and Waypoint shows its live status, departure and arrival times, terminals and gates for the day you are travelling.

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO
Check the weather for your destination, convert currencies between any two currencies with live exchange rates, and read a short summary about the places you are visiting.

DISCOVER NEARBY
Explore restaurants, attractions and more near your destination, and add the places you like straight to your itinerary.

NEVER MISS A THING
Get reminders before your trip and alerts about your flights as a push notification.

WORKS OFFLINE, SYNCS WHEN YOU ARE BACK
Your trips are stored on your phone, so they open without a signal. When you reconnect, changes sync to your account and are restored automatically on a new phone.

BUILT FOR YOU

* Sign in with Google or with an email and password
* Lock the app with your fingerprint or face
* Light and dark themes, with a switch in your profile
* Available in English, isiZulu and isiXhosa

Waypoint is free and has no ads.
